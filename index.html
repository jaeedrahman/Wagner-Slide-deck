Responsibilities · Import

From Wechingen to Chattogram

1

Direct lifting

The sole distributor orders and lifts product directly from Wagner in Germany.

2

Payment by LC

Payment to Wagner through a 100% letter of credit at sight.

3

Shipment to port

Wagner ships to the distributor's local port. Freight is added to the product cost.

Customs duty, clearance and all other port expenses are handled by the sole distributor without delay.

Responsibilities · Distribution

Coverage in every division, district and thana

Local distribution

Set up local distributors across all divisions, districts and thana markets. Minimum 50 distributors. Wagner may audit every market.

Manpower

Staff operations to industry norms and Wagner's advice.

Product placement

Place product with retailers across the country, monitored by the distributor's team. Wagner audits placement every quarter.

Responsibilities · B2C & e-commerce

Marketing

Sales target

One 40 ft container (26,000 L) a month in the first quarter, then two (52,000 L) a month from the second quarter. Volume rises with market demand.

Annual target & incentive

30 containers (≈ 8 lakh litres) a year. On reaching it, the distributor earns 1% of total invoice value at year end.

Growth

Minimum 25% growth a year in quantity purchased.

Two months of stock, always rolling
